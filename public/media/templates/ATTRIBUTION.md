## Reference collection

The 85 reference-inspired templates use local Pexels photographs listed in
`editorial/sources.json`. Each entry records its source URL, license and pixel
dimensions. The images are incorporated into editable compositions.

Fonts are served from `/fonts/display/`; `editorial-sources.json` records the
download sources and each family has its SIL Open Font License included.
Full category sizes, regeneration instructions and fidelity limitations are
documented in `docs/reference-template-collection.md` in the project.
